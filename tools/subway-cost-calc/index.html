Why is the Climate Card not always the answer?

The pass buys unlimited rides, so it only wins when the rides you would otherwise pay for cost more than the pass. Two rides a day for four days is ₩12,400 on T-money, less than the 5-day pass at ₩15,000 — the pass loses. Four rides a day for four days is ₩24,800, and the pass wins by ₩9,800. The table above does this sum for your numbers.

Do the numbers include the airport leg in the pass?

No. The pass cannot be used to board at Incheon T1 or T2, so the airport leg is always paid separately, whatever the daily plan. The totals add it: all-stop train by default, or the express or limousine if that plan says so.

What is not in the total?

Taxis, the KTX, GTX, the Shinbundang Line, red intercity buses and anything outside Seoul's city limits. A data-only eSIM and food are separate too. Airport buses and taxis take a T-money card; GTX and the Shinbundang Line take a card but not the Seoul pass.
