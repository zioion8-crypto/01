1Airport → city: three doors, three prices

Incheon is 50 km out. Which door you take decides the first hour of the trip, and one of the three is a trap the first time you meet it.

Buy at the booth or the machine in the arrivals hall. No reservation, no app, no Korean phone number — this is the door to take if your phone is dead or your eSIM has not activated yet.

The trap: a Climate Card cannot be used to board at Incheon T1/T2 — those stations sit outside the Seoul network. Take AREX or the bus into the city first, then load the pass.

Four things the Climate Card does not cover — this is where most refunds of goodwill are lost:
① GTX A / B / C — separate fare on every section, the gate will not open.
② Shinbundang Line (Gangnam ↔ Pangyo/Suwon) — privately run, separate fare.
③ Red intercity buses and airport limousine buses (Nami Island, Everland, Suwon, the 6001/6015 airport buses) — the card stops working the moment you leave the city, and only Seoul-licensed buses are covered.
④ Boarding at Incheon T1/T2, and AREX beyond Gimpo Airport–Seoul Station — exit only at the airport stations; you may get off there if you boarded inside Seoul.

The 440 machines that take a foreign card

Since 2026-03-17, ticket machines on lines 1–8 — 273 stations, 440 machines — accept foreign-issued Visa and Mastercard with an average 3.7% surcharge, plus KakaoPay and NaverPay. On those machines you can buy a single ticket, buy the ₩3,000 Climate Card, and load the 1/2/3/5/7-day pass.

Everywhere else, the rule is still the old one: loading a transit card means cash in won. Station machines and convenience stores take won notes, not your card. Plan one ATM stop, not five.

Tap out, always. Touch the card for a full half-second on the way out too. If you only tap in, the next ride is charged at the maximum distance — up to ₩1,400 extra — and your transfer discount is gone. To empty a T-money card at the end of a trip, any convenience store refunds a balance up to ₩20,000 for a ₩500 fee.

3Tax refund: two doors, one bracket table

Korea refunds VAT at the counter, not at the airport. That is the whole difference from Japan, where the refund moved to after departure on 2026-11-01. If you only learn one thing: ask at the till.

Door 1 — instant refund at the till

  1. Minimum ₩15,000 per store, under ₩1,000,000 per purchase, and no more than ₩5,000,000 across your whole stay.
  2. Hand over your passport original — a photo on your phone is refused. The VAT comes off the bill immediately: no airport queue, no form.
  3. Look for the Tax Free sticker, and remember registration is per branch: the same brand can refund in Myeongdong and not in a mall outlet. Ask before you queue at the register.

Door 2 — claim it later

The ₩1,000 trap: a ₩75,000 purchase refunds ₩5,000. A ₩74,000 purchase refunds ₩3,000. One thousand won less spent, two thousand won less back. If you are at ₩73,000 and there is anything you actually need, add it — and never split one shopping run into two receipts at the same store.

Beauty procedures: the VAT refund on cosmetic procedures ended for supplies from 2025-12-31. Any page still saying "dermatology is refundable" is out of date. Hotel refunds are under a 2027-06-30 sunset proposal — treat as not available today.

4Where payment fails, and the ladder up

Korea is close to cashless, but the failures cluster in predictable places. Learn the ladder once: card → another terminal → cash → a person.

PlaceWhat happensWhat to do
Unmanned kiosks, self-checkout, photo booths, lockersThe most common refusal in the country. They want a Korean card or coins.Have ₩5,000 in coins/notes, or find the staffed counter. Never stand there re-tapping.

No tipping. Not in restaurants, not in taxis, not in hotels. The price on the menu is the price you pay. If a receipt shows a service charge at a large restaurant, that is the house's, not yours to add to.

5eSIM: the internet is easy, the 010 number is not

A travel eSIM solves data in five minutes. It does not solve identity — and in Korea, identity is a phone number.

You getYou do not get
Mobile data from the moment you land, hotspot sharing, no shop visit, keeps your own number active via dual SIM.A Korean 010 number. That number is what unlocks: Tableling reservations, most Korean restaurant queues, delivery apps (Baemin, Coupang Eats), loyalty sign-ups, and in many cases Kakao T.

If you want to book a restaurant without a Korean number

  1. Catchtable GLOBAL — same restaurant list, sign in with Google/Apple/email, deposit paid by foreign card. The safest route.
  2. Naver reservation — opened to passport holders in June 2026; you upload the passport and reserve without a 010.
  3. Walk in early, or ask your hotel front desk to call. Front desks do this all day.
  4. If you truly need a number, buy a voice eSIM or SIM with an 010 number — data-only plans never have one.

One practical consequence: if a guide or a booking page promises "we will reserve it for you", that is a travel-agency service in Korea and needs a licence. What is sold legitimately is information and the sentence to say at the counter — which is what the guides below are.

6Maps: Google still cannot walk you there

As of November 2026, Google Maps has no walking or driving directions in Korea. The government's conditional approval of the map-data export was confirmed on 2026-11-13, with full routing targeted for Q1 2027. Until then — and afterwards, for the last 300 m — the key to the map is the Korean name.

  1. Install Naver Map, set the language to English in settings.
  2. Copy the Korean name below and paste it into search. Do not search in English — it will land on a different branch, or a closed one.
  3. Choose walk or transit; the exit number appears in the first step of the route.
