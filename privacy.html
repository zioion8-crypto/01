Privacy Policy

Korea Travel Bible · Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Contact: support@koreatravelbible.com

1. Information We Collect

Korea Travel Bible (koreatravelbible.com) respects your privacy. Our static guide pages do not require user accounts and ask you for no personal details to read them. The only information a visit produces is the aggregate visit statistics described in section 2.

When you purchase a digital Product or contact customer support, our Merchant of Record (such as Creem) or email system collects only the information necessary to process your order, deliver your digital files, comply with tax obligations, and respond to your support request (such as your name, email address, and country/region for tax calculation).

2. Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

Every page on this site loads one analytics tag: Google Analytics 4 (GA4), gtag.js from www.googletagmanager.com, measurement ID G-LZJCQWM2YS. It tells us, in aggregate, which pages and which free PDF downloads are useful so we can keep them accurate. We do not send your name, email address or account details, and nothing you type into the tools (the order card you build, the days and rides you enter in a calculator, the places you save) is sent to us or to Google.

GA4 may set its own first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to tell a returning browser from a new one. Google receives the page address, the referring page, device and browser type, an approximate location derived from the connection (not your GPS position), and a random client identifier. We do not use it for advertising, do not enable Google Signals or ad personalisation, and do not combine it with order or email records.

You can refuse it: block or delete cookies for koreatravelbible.com in your browser, browse in a private window, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Every page keeps working without it. Google's own handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy.

3. Payment Processing

We do not store credit card numbers or sensitive payment credentials on our servers. All payment transactions are handled securely by our Merchant of Record in accordance with PCI-DSS standards.

4. How We Use Information

  • To deliver purchased digital guides, map packs, and product updates.
  • To respond to customer support and refund inquiries sent to support@koreatravelbible.com.
  • To comply with applicable legal and tax requirements.

5. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Visit statistics are processed by Google as a service provider under its own terms; order and support records are retained only as long as necessary to provide customer support and meet legal obligations.

6. Your Rights & Contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your support correspondence at any time by emailing support@koreatravelbible.com.
