4. Four things that catch people

  • The fast ferry is gone for good. Plan around the New Camellia’s monthly calendar, or fly from Gimhae.
  • Google Maps will fail you in Korea. No walking or driving directions; use Naver Map with the Korean name.
  • Two plugs. Korea 220 V type C/F, Japan 100 V type A. A phone charger is fine; a hair dryer is not.
  • Departure taxes. Japan ¥3,000 International Tourist Tax on every departure since 1 July 2026; Korea ₩7,000 by air, ₩1,000 by sea.

4. Cuatro cosas que sorprenden

  • El ferri rápido ya no existe. Organiza con el calendario mensual del New Camellia o vuela desde Gimhae.
  • Google Maps te falla en Corea. Sin rutas a pie ni en coche; usa Naver Map con el nombre en coreano.
