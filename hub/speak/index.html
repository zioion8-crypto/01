Speak & Show

Checked Korean lines by situation. Tap one and the screen becomes one sentence in big type — turn the phone around and let them read it.

Show the screen rather than reading it out: the person in front of you reads Korean far faster than you can pronounce it. Allergy lines come first and are written the way kitchens read them.

    Not a translation service. These are fixed, reviewed lines — there is no machine translation here. For anything outside them, the free order card builds more sentences.

    A card lowers the risk of a misunderstanding; it does not remove it. For a serious allergy, show the line before you order, and use a translation app for anything medical.
